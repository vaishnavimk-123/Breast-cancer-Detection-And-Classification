    Imputation: SimpleImputer(strategy='median') handles missing or omitted clinical values.

    Feature Selection: SelectKBest(score_func=f_classif, k=10) selects the 10 top ANOVA-discriminating biomarkers.

    Standardization: StandardScaler() standardizes features by removing mean and scaling to unit variance.

    Estimator: LogisticRegression(max_iter=5000, random_state=42) produces calibrated probability scores.

Clinical Background: Fine Needle Aspirate (FNA) Cytopathology

Breast cancer is among the most prevalent malignancies globally. Fine Needle Aspiration (FNA) cytology is an established minimally-invasive diagnostic procedure utilized in the triple assessment (physical examination, mammography/ultrasound imaging, and cytology) of palpable breast masses.

The Wisconsin Breast Cancer Diagnostic Dataset features were computed from a high-resolution digitized image of a fine needle aspirate (FNA) of a breast mass. They describe characteristics of the cell nuclei present in the image:

  • Radius: Mean of distances from center to points on perimeter.
  • Texture: Standard deviation of gray-scale values.
  • Perimeter & Area: Nuclear size parameters correlating with cellular hypertrophy.
  • Smoothness: Local variation in radius lengths.
  • Compactness: Perimeter² / Area - 1.0 (shape irregularity indicator).
  • Concavity: Severity of concave portions of the contour.
  • Concave Points: Number of concave portions of the contour.
  • Symmetry: Bilateral nuclear symmetry.
  • Fractal Dimension: "Coastline approximation" - 1 (boundary complexity).

Clinical Decision Support Triage Protocol

Low Risk (< 25% Probability): Features resemble benign fibroadenoma or cystic changes. Standard routine screening per age protocol.
Indeterminate / Borderline Risk (25% - 60%): Elevated morphological atypia near decision boundaries. Recommended follow-up: Repeat FNA, core needle biopsy, or multidisciplinary review.
High Risk (> 60% Probability): Significant nuclear enlargement, high concavity, and pleomorphism indicative of invasive carcinoma. Prompt histological confirmation and oncology referral advised.
